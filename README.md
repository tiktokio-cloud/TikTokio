# TikTokio

[![TikTokio logo](https://tiktokio.cloud/assets/logo-cloud.svg?v=20261023-mobile-menu-languages)](https://tiktokio.cloud)

[![TikTokio favicon](https://tiktokio.cloud/assets/favicon.png)](https://tiktokio.cloud)

TikTokio is a free browser-based TikTok video downloader. According to the website, it lets users save TikTok videos in MP4 format with or without a watermark, and it can also extract audio as MP3. The site says the tool works without installing an app and is available on Android, iOS, iPhone, iPad, PC, Mac, Windows, macOS, and Linux through a modern browser.

The website presents TikTokio as a simple downloader that preserves the source quality of the content. It says downloads can reach resolutions up to 1080 pixels, depending on the original TikTok upload and the option selected. TikTokio also states that the service is SSL encrypted and does not require personal account credentials or payment details.

## Available download formats

TikTokio lists three main output options:

- **MP4 video without watermark** — a clean video file without the TikTok logo and username overlay.
- **MP4 video with watermark** — a video file that keeps the original watermark.
- **MP3 audio** — the audio track extracted from the TikTok clip.

The website says the downloader preserves the source quality and supports resolutions up to 1080 pixels. Actual quality depends on the original TikTok upload and the option selected.

## How to download a TikTok video

TikTokio outlines a three-step process:

1. **Copy the link.** Open the TikTok app, find the video, tap Share, then tap Copy Link.
2. **Paste into TikTokio.** Open TikTokio in a modern browser, paste the video link into the box, and click Download.
3. **Choose your format.** Select a suitable option from the multiple options shown and download the video.

The site also gives instructions for PC and Mac users. Open a browser such as Chrome, Edge, Safari, or Opera, go to TikTok, find the video, and click the share button. Click Copy Link, open TikTokio in a new tab, paste the link into the URL bar, and click Download. According to the site, the video should be downloaded within seconds.

For iPhone and iPad users, TikTokio says the downloader is versatile and can be used on iOS devices to download TikTok videos without a watermark.

## MP3 audio downloads

TikTokio also explains how to save audio as MP3. The process begins in the TikTok app: choose a video, tap Share, and copy the link. Next, open the TikTok downloader, paste the link into the box, and click Download. From the available options, choose Download MP3. The audio file should then be saved to the device within seconds.

This feature is presented as a way to extract audio from a clip. Users should still respect the rights of the original creator when using downloaded audio.

## Common issues and troubleshooting

The site lists several common problems and fixes:

- **Check the video link.** A broken link prevents TikTokio from fetching data. Make sure the copied URL is correct and the video is public, not private.
- **Use a stable connection.** Unstable internet can cause slow processing or long downloads. Connect to a stable internet connection.
- **Clear your browser cache.** If buffering or lagging occurs, clear the browser cache, reload TikTokio, and try again.
- **Check browser extensions.** Chrome extensions or ad blockers can affect the download button. Close ad blockers or other extensions while using the site.
